## Changed
- **Segmented controls and joined button groups use 1px ink dividers** inside their 2px frame. Before, the dividers were 2px. A range bar such as 1h / 24h / 7d / 30d / 90d / More read as a heavy grid that outweighed the selected option. The CSS change is in `the-mechanics.css`; markup stays the same.
- **Rule** (DESIGN.md Segmented control, skill `references/rules.md` §1): before drawing a divider, ask whether the cells are several things or several values of one thing.
  - Cells with their own content and state are separate objects and keep **2px** dividers. Examples: panel grid, KPI tiles, product cards, pipeline stages; several of them can be "on" at once.
  - Options of one control are parts of one object and get **1px** dividers. Examples: segmented control, joined button group; exactly one is chosen.

## Apply in your app
Nothing to change if the app uses `.tm-segmented` / `.tm-btn-group`. If the app draws its own option strip (custom classes or inline borders), give the lines between options 1px ink (`var(--tm-bw-3) solid var(--tm-ink)`) and keep the 2px outer frame.

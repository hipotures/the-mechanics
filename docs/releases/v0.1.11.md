One page width for every app: content is capped and centered by the library, not by each app.

## Changed
- **`.tm-main` centers page content at `--tm-content-max` (1600px).** The main area keeps its full width (background, scrolling); its side padding grows so the content is at most 1600px wide and centered in the space next to the sidebar. The sidebar stays at the left edge. Below about 1880px window width nothing changes.
- **New modifiers:** `.tm-main--narrow` caps at `--tm-content-narrow` (1120px) for forms, settings and reading pages; `.tm-main--full` removes the cap for views that use every column.
- **New `.tm-container`** (and `.tm-container--narrow`) applies the same cap to layouts without `.tm-shell`.
- The skill and `DESIGN.md` ("Content width") state the rule: no app-specific `max-width` on page content, never left-aligned on wide screens.

## Apply in your app
1. Re-run the installer (below).
2. Remove your own `max-width` / `margin: auto` / centered grid wrappers around page content.
3. With the shell: keep content directly in `.tm-main` (add `--narrow` or `--full` where it fits). Without the shell: wrap the page content in `<div class="tm-container">`.

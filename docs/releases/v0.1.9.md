Behaviors ship as `the-mechanics.js`, and the demo is live on GitHub Pages.

## New
- **`the-mechanics.js`**: an optional ES module with no dependencies, installed next to the CSS. CSS cannot restyle what the browser draws itself, so this module replaces it:
  - date and time fields: our `.tm-picker` (Monday first, 24 h) instead of the browser's calendar or clock;
  - selects in browsers without `base-select` (Firefox): our list instead of the OS list;
  - `data-tm-tip` tooltips instead of `title`;
  - `confirmDialog()` instead of `confirm()`.
- It also brings the behaviors the design rules assume:
  - menus: anchored to their button, arrow keys, focus return, following their field on page scroll;
  - the mouse/keyboard modality hook, the ✕ in text fields, steppers;
  - `toast()`, `dismissibleAlert()`, `validateInline()` / `setFieldError()`.
- The module starts itself and also enhances elements added later. Classic scripts reach it as `window.TheMechanics`. To start it yourself, put `data-tm-manual` on `<html>` and call `init(root)`. Details: `distribution.md` › Behaviors.
- **Live demo:** https://hipotures.github.io/the-mechanics/ (published on every release).
- The source audit finds two new problems:
  - `own-helper-copy`: the app has its own copy of one of these behaviors;
  - `no-behaviors-js`: the app has date/time fields or selects but never loads the module.

## Changed
- `confirmDialog({ body })` is now plain text; pass trusted markup as `bodyHTML`. Focus returns to the element that opened the dialog.
- `toast()` creates `.tm-toast-region` if the page has none. Danger and warning toasts stay until closed unless you pass `timeout`.

## Apply in your app
1. Re-run the installer (below). It puts `the-mechanics.js` next to the CSS.
2. Load it once on every page: `<script type="module" src="/vendor/the-mechanics/the-mechanics.js"></script>`. Adjust the path to where your server serves the vendored files.
3. **Delete the copies you made of playground helpers** and use the module instead:
   - `tm-select.js` or ported `enhanceSelects`, `enhanceDateTime`, `followAnchor`, `trackInputModality`, `anchorPopover`, `enhanceTips`, `enhanceClearable`;
   - your own confirm dialog: call `window.TheMechanics.confirmDialog({ title, body, confirm, danger })`, or import it.
   - Keep app-specific code, such as toasts that flag their record.
4. Check every date or time field, including those in menus and dialogs. It must open our calendar or clock, not the browser's. The skill's audits report what is left:
   - `node .claude/skills/the-mechanics/scripts/audit.mjs <src>`
   - `node .claude/skills/the-mechanics/scripts/ui-audit.mjs <url>`
